02B / RESEARCH NOTE

From environmental pressure
to development pathways.

The project combines environmental indicators with socio-economic outcomes to examine how country profiles differ and change across time.

01
DATA

195-country sample

Environmental and socio-economic indicators were assembled into a reproducible cross-national dataset covering 1990–2023.

World Bank · FAOSTAT · WHO GHO · ILOSTAT · UNDP HDR
02
METHOD

Planetary Pressure Index

Six environmental indicators were directionally coded, standardised and combined into a composite pressure index, followed by K-means clustering.

Python · pandas · scikit-learn · statistical analysis
03
INTERPRETATION

Four configurations

The analysis distinguishes High Pressure / Low Development, High Pressure / High Development, Low Pressure / Low Development, and Transitioning / Decoupler profiles.

Cluster profiles · transition matrices · regression analysis

Inclusive Hiring in

SurveyCTOPythonGoogle Apps ScriptRCT DesignSamplingField Research
What I built

Survey instruments with logical flows and error checks; an automated CV-generation workflow; field-surveyor training and sampling support; literature review and research documentation.

How the RCT was structured

Candidate eligibility → random assignment → job-feature interventions around gender ratio, ergonomic support and safety → application, acceptance and retention outcomes → planned statistical analysis.
