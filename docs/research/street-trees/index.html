Do New Street Trees Cool Baltimore?

Do newly planted trees make a street cooler, or were greener places already cooling? I compared nearby blocks over time. The evidence points to a small drop in summer surface temperature as young trees grow.

0.026 °C less surface heat per young treeEstimated for 2022–25 · Not a measure of air temperature
