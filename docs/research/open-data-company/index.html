Open Data Company

A free site that takes someone from “I need data on X” to a number they can cite or a clean file they can use, in a few clicks. Ask for a number in plain words and get the value, its year and its source. Or take a whole dataset: choose the variables, places and years, check the table and download it. No sign-up.

  • Ask: one number, a comparison, a ranking or a world series, each with its source, a chart and a citation.
  • Explorer: pick variables, places and years, merge datasets, check the table, then download CSV, Excel, JSON or TSV.
  • Researcher mode recommends a statistical test for a stated hypothesis and explains why. Your data profiles a local file in the browser without uploading it.

9 datasets · 284 variables · 118,219 rows · Checked every day · Statistics tested against SciPy and statsmodels
