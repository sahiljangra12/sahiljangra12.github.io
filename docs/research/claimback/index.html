ClaimBack

Fight a health insurance denial in minutes, not hours. Upload a denial letter or Explanation of Benefits; ClaimBack explains why the claim was denied, gives the appeal deadline, lists the evidence that usually wins and drafts an appeal letter to edit and send.

  • Insurers on HealthCare.gov denied 19% of in-network claims in 2024, yet fewer than 1% of denials are appealed (KFF analysis of CMS data).
  • Reads PDFs and photos, finds claim details and CARC reason codes, and sorts the denial into one of 12 categories.
  • Federal appeal windows by plan type: employer (ERISA), Marketplace, Medicaid and Medicare Advantage.

Python · Streamlit · Tesseract OCR · Browser version runs on the device · General information, not legal or medical advice
