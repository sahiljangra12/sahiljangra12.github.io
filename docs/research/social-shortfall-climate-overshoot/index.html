Can basic needs be met within the climate boundary?

A country-year panel comparing four social thresholds (life expectancy, under-5 mortality, electricity access and employment) with consumption-based CO2, measured against a fair-share boundary of 1.61 t per person per year.

  • No country with consumption-based estimates met all four social thresholds within the boundary in any year.
  • The pooled-trend carbon requirement fell from about 26 t (1991) to 8.5 t (2022), still 5.3 times the boundary.
  • Absolute decoupling spread to 48 countries in 2011–2022, but only 2 of 41 decouplers above the boundary cut fast enough to reach it by 2050.

Python · Panel data · Logistic models · Decoupling analysis · Descriptive associations, not causal effects
