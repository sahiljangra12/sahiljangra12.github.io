# sahiljangra12.github.io

Personal research site of **Sahil** — data science and research on sustainability, climate, health and development (Johns Hopkins University, MSE Data Science).

Live at **https://sahiljangra12.github.io**

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step needed to serve. `python3 build.py` regenerates the three pages from the content in that file.
- Section artwork in `assets/img/art/` is computer-rendered illustration, not study results.
- Papers in `papers/` are copied from each project's own repository.
